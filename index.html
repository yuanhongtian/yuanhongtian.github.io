NONLINEAR SCHRÖDINGER EQUATION

Localized perturbations of the nonlinear Schrödinger equation

We study the one-dimensional defocusing cubic NLS equation with a localized higher-order nonlinearity. Improved L∞ bounds and Lp a priori estimates extend the range of perturbations we can treat and show, under the assumptions of the paper, that the perturbed equation retains the long-time asymptotic behavior of its integrable counterpart.

Letters in Mathematical Physics, 2025 ↘

MODIFIED KORTEWEG–DE VRIES EQUATION

Long-time asymptotics for perturbed mKdV

We derive full long-time asymptotics for the modified KdV equation with a higher-order perturbation. The analysis extends the perturbative approach for integrable systems to mKdV, combining inverse scattering, nonlinear steepest descent, and a priori estimates under the stated assumptions on the initial data and perturbation.

Journal of Nonlinear Science, 2026 ↘
